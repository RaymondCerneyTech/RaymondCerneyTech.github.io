How I approach problems

Understand the system before changing it

I work best when structured responsibilities also include investigation, variation, troubleshooting, learning, or opportunities to improve the process.

1 · Map

Understand the system

Identify the inputs, outputs, constraints, dependencies, and rules that shape how the work currently operates.

2 · Compare

Find the meaningful difference

Separate minor variation from the discrepancy that actually changes quality, behavior, risk, or outcome.

3 · Test

Evaluate alternate routes

Question assumptions, compare approaches, and look for strategies that are more efficient or more reliable than the default.
